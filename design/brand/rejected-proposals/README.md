# Rejected brand proposals

> **None of the files in this folder are the Not at Home brand.**
> Do not use these logos, colors or fonts in the product.
> The final brand identity is documented in [`design/brand/README.md`](../README.md), and the logo files to use are in [`design/brand/logo/`](../logo/).

These are the first three directions explored on 2026-10-14, kept only as a record of what was considered.

| Proposal | Concept | Colors | Why it was not chosen |
|---|---|---|---|
| [A: Summit](proposal-a-summit/palette.md) | Mountain range with a snow cap and a sun, name underneath | Pine `#1F4D3A`, Ember `#E06A2C` | The palette was kept for the final design, but not the logo. |
| [B: Treeline](proposal-b-treeline/palette.md) | Name standing on a plain with pines and a rising sun | Lake `#1C3B5A`, Ochre `#E3A72F` | Not selected. |
| [C: Trailhead](proposal-c-trailhead/palette.md) | Two peaks with a winding trail cut to the summit | Rust `#B4472A`, Glacier `#3D6F8E` | The mark was kept and reworked for the final design, but not these colors. |

Open `preview.html` to see them side by side.

The final design went through two more rounds that are not kept here: a mixed-case wordmark ("Not at Home" / "Stories from the trail"), dropped in favor of all caps, and an earlier version of the final mark with steeper, plain triangular peaks.
