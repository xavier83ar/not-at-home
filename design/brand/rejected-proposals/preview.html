Rejected brand proposals

Rejected proposals. None of these is the Not at Home brand. They are the first three directions explored before the final design, kept for reference only. The final logo, colors and fonts are documented in design/brand/README.md.

A

Summit

A mountain range with a split-off snow cap and a low sun, with the name set underneath in a condensed, gear-label style. The closest to Columbia and The North Face.

Barlow Condensed Bold for the name, Barlow SemiBold for the slogan.
